## 2. Visual design choices

- **Two-panel contrast:** Mirrors a before/after story (problem vs solution), which matches 3MT storytelling rubric.
- **Color coding:** Warm red tones for overload; green/blue for coordinated flow—readable on projectors.
- **Typography:** Large title; short panel titles; minimal text so judges read less and listen more.
- **Bottom bar:** Name + research line + one-sentence insight tying metaphor to research (efficient execution for structured LLM programs).

---

## 3. Story arc (spoken, not on slide)

1. **Hook:** Have you ever waited too long for an AI answer while the service seems busy?  
2. **Stakes:** Behind the scenes, many requests repeat the same long setup work.  
3. **Metaphor:** A chaotic kitchen vs a kitchen that batches similar orders and shares prep.  
4. **Pivot to research:** My work studies how to organize these AI workflows so systems reuse work safely and scale better.  
5. **Outcome / significance:** That means faster responses and lower cost for real deployments—without changing the underlying model.  
6. **Close:** One sentence invitation: happy to explain more after this.
